— WELCOME TO REVATHY DENTISTREE —

Redefining Excellence in Oral Healthcare

Welcome to Revathy Dentistree, where visionary dental care meets world-class expertise. We are thrilled to welcome you to our brand-new, expansive, state-of-the-art facility — designed from the ground up to elevate your experience and deliver the next generation of dental and maxillofacial care.

Your smile is our absolute priority. Our upgraded, larger clinical space blends a calming, luxurious environment with cutting-edge diagnostic and treatment technologies, ensuring that every visit is seamless, precise, and entirely comfortable.

Expanded, State-of-the-Art Facility

A new, larger clinic with advanced infrastructure and modern dental chairs — a spacious, hygienic environment built for optimal patient comfort.

Advanced Technology & Precision

From high-resolution digital imaging to ultra-modern surgical tools, we use elite technology for accurate diagnoses and predictable outcomes.

A Compassionate, Expert Team

Led by experienced specialists who pair clinical mastery with a warm, personalised approach to keep your smile pristine for life.

Advanced Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery

A dedicated, highly specialised setup for complex surgical interventions:

  • Wisdom tooth extractions & impacted tooth management
  • Facial trauma care
  • Corrective jaw surgeries
  • Comprehensive management of oral pathologies
  • Precision dental implant placements

Comprehensive & Specialized Dental Care

A complete spectrum of dental services under one roof:

  • Preventive dentistry — deep cleaning, sealants & oral screenings
  • Restorative & endodontics — aesthetic fillings & painless root canals
  • Cosmetic & smile design — laser whitening, veneers & digital previews
  • Braces & aligners for all age groups
  • Paediatric dental care
  • Dentures, aesthetic crowns (Zirconia, E-Max, PFM) & bridges
